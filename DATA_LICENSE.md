# Data licence

The published index series in `data/outputs/` (KMSI, KBRI, KCEI scores, components and release records) are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
You may reuse them for any purpose, including commercially, provided you cite the source as given in `CITATION.cff`.

Third-party input data keep their original terms. Files from providers whose terms restrict redistribution
(Yahoo Finance, AAII) are not included; `src/ingest` downloads them and `data/raw/manifest.json` records their hashes.
